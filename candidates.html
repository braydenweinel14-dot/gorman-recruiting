Three useful next steps.

  1. Outline what you’re looking for.

    Note your experience, strengths, work preferences, availability, and location.

  2. Ask about current opportunities.

    Call (812) 618-9911 or use the original website’s contact section (new tab). Ask how to share a résumé and which roles are currently available.

    Ask what documents you need, how interviews work, whether any fees apply, and how follow-up will happen.
