Privacy policy.

Learn what this website concept stores, how external services work, and how to contact the business about information you share.

Last updated: October 15, 2026.

About this notice

This notice describes this website concept. It does not describe the data practices of Google, social platforms, or the hosting provider.

Information collected by this site

This site has no application or inquiry submission system. It does not collect résumés, names, email addresses, or messages through a form. We have not installed Google Analytics, advertising pixels, or advertising cookies.

Display preferences

If you use the reading options, your browser’s local storage remembers your larger-text and contrast settings under the key gorman-reading-options. The site does not send these preferences to a business database. Use “Reset display” or clear your browser’s site data to reset them.

Hosting and external services

The hosting provider may process technical connection information to serve and protect the site. Fonts load from Google Fonts. A Google map loads only when you select “Load Google map.” Those services receive connection information when requested. External services apply their own policies.

Links, calls, and sharing

Telephone links open your calling application. Directions, independent reviews, and social-sharing links open external services. Using them may share information with the selected service. The device share button opens a chooser; copying a link places the page URL on your clipboard.

Information you give the business

Contacting Gorman by phone or another agreed channel is separate from browsing this website. Ask the business directly about how it uses and retains your information, or how to request changes or deletion.

Questions

For questions about information shared with Gorman Recruiting, call (812) 618-9911 or use contact details. Do not submit sensitive documents through social-sharing tools.
