FOR CANDIDATES / YOUR NEXT STEPS

A clearer conversation.

Whether you’re starting out, returning to work, or considering a change, use this guide to organize what you want to share.

  1. 01

    Think about what fits.

    Write down your experience, strengths, preferred location, availability, and the kind of work you want to explore.

  2. 02

    Contact the team.

    Call (812) 618-9911 or use View contact options. Ask about current openings and how to share a résumé.

  3. 03

    Confirm what happens next.

    Ask about the application steps, what information to prepare, any candidate fees, and when to expect an update.

Need another way to communicate?

Tell the team your preferred way to communicate and ask what interview or application support is available. For help making a phone call, you may use a relay service or ask someone you trust to assist. Arrangements must be confirmed directly with Gorman.
